Features

🎙️

Dictate from your wrist

Press the button, speak, done. Brain Dump uses Pebble's built-in microphone — no extra hardware needed.

⚡

Quick launch

Assign Brain Dump as a quick-launch action on your watch face — one long press and you're dictating.

🧠

Smart routing

NLP classifies your note on-device in milliseconds and sends it to the right place automatically.

🤖

Ask your AI

Ask anything and get a concise reply on your watch. Works with NVIDIA, Groq, Ollama, Mistral, OpenRouter, and any OpenAI-compatible API.

📴

Works offline

Notes go to on-watch local reminders when there's no connection — nothing gets lost.

🔒

No account required

All routing logic runs locally on your phone. Bring your own keys for the services you already use.

How it works

1

Press the button on your Pebble

The dictation session starts immediately. Speak your note naturally.

2

Brain Dump classifies your intent

"Remind me to call John" → Tasks. "What's the capital of Japan?" → AI. "Idea: dark mode toggle" → Notion.

3

Your note lands in the right place

Google Tasks, Notion, a custom webhook, or your on-watch list — instantly, without touching your phone.

4

AI replies appear on your watch

Concise answers formatted for the small screen. Follow-up questions keep context from the previous exchange.
